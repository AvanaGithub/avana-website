What is Osteoarthritis?

Osteoarthritis (OA) of the knee is the gradual wearing away of the smooth cartilage that cushions the ends of your bones. As it wears, one side of the knee joint takes on more load than it should — leading to pain, stiffness and swelling with everyday movement.

Left unmanaged, the pain worsens and simple tasks — walking, climbing stairs, standing from a chair — become difficult. But there is a middle step between painkillers and surgery.

The Real Offloading Knee Brace

In knee osteoarthritis, most of the pain comes from one worn side of the joint carrying too much load. The Freestyle OA gently shifts that load away from the painful side — a proven principle called offloading.

Less pressure on the damaged cartilage means less pain with every step. It's lightweight, worn under or over clothing, and made by Breg — a global leader in orthopedic bracing.

Most patients with early to moderate knee osteoarthritis experience noticeable pain relief within the first few weeks. Individual results vary — the best way to know is our 7-Day Comfort Promise. Please consult your doctor to confirm it's right for your condition.

The Freestyle OA is a non-invasive external brace made by Breg, USA — a global leader in orthopedic bracing. There are no medications or surgery involved. Some patients feel mild adjustment discomfort in the first few days, which typically settles as you get used to the fit.

A knee replacement is major surgery that replaces the joint. The Freestyle OA is worn externally — it shifts pressure away from the worn side of your knee so you feel less pain during daily movement. For many patients it helps postpone surgery, sometimes by years.
