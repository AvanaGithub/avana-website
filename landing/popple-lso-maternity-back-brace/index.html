The problem

Pregnancy changes everything — including your back.

Up to half of all women experience lower-back and pelvic girdle pain during pregnancy, especially in the third trimester. As hormones loosen ligaments and your centre of gravity shifts forward, stress builds on the lumbar spine, SI joints and pelvis — causing low back pain, pelvic pain and round-ligament pain.

Most belly bands offer little real structure. Popple was built differently — to address the biomechanics, not just cover the bump.

Simple to use

How to wear it

  • Select your size by measuring around the waist.
  • Apply the fully-extended brace with the back panel centred and low on the back.
  • Wrap to support the abdomen, right side over left.
  • Slip thumbs through the pull tabs and adjust compression, then secure.
  • Loosen or tighten anytime as your comfort changes through the day.
