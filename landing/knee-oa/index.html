Realistic hope · 1 of 4

OA doesn't reverse. But the load on your knee can change.

Osteoarthritis slowly thins the cartilage that cushions your knee. In most people it starts on the inner side. Nothing regrows that cartilage.

The problem · 2 of 4

Every step presses bone onto the worn spot.

What you can change is how much weight lands on that spot with every step — and that is often where much of the pain comes from. A knee cap only wraps the joint.

The mechanism · 3 of 4

A Load-Shift brace moves the weight.

A rigid hinge on one side and the straps nudge the knee into slightly better alignment as you walk. Less load on the worn side means less pain where it hurts most.

Braces that shift load in the main knee joint are strongly recommended in the 2019 ACR / Arthritis Foundation osteoarthritis guideline.

You're in control · 4 of 4

Turn the dial. Feel the load come off.

Just turn the thumbwheel on the hinge — no tools — and the brace takes more weight off the painful side as you feel it. Our team sets it for you at fitting. The single slim hinge sits comfortably under trousers, and the wrap keeps the knee warm.

Is it right for you

Who Load-Shift Bracing suits

Often a good fit

  • OA mainly on one side of the knee, usually the inner side
  • Mild to moderate OA, often grade 2 or 3 on X-ray
  • You want to keep walking, working and doing daily tasks

Talk to your doctor first if

  • OA affects the whole knee badly (end-stage)
  • Your pain is mainly at the front, around the kneecap
  • You have poor circulation or skin problems around the knee

The best results come from combining steps — exercise and physiotherapy, weight management, bracing, and medicines or injections where your doctor advises. Surgery is for advanced OA when other steps aren't enough.

FAQ

Questions people ask us

How is this different from a knee cap?

A knee cap gives warmth and a feeling of support. It doesn't change where your weight goes. A Load-Shift brace has a rigid hinge that moves load off the worn side.

Will it cure my OA?

No. Nothing reverses cartilage loss. A Load-Shift brace aims to reduce pain and make walking easier while you follow your doctor's plan.

Can it help me avoid knee replacement?

Some people with mild to moderate OA stay comfortable for years with bracing, exercise and weight management. Advanced OA may still need surgery. Your orthopaedic surgeon is the right person to judge your stage.

How is it fitted?

See a doctor straight away if

  • Severe pain at rest, or pain that wakes you at night
  • The knee locks or keeps giving way
  • Swelling that won't settle with rest, ice and raising the leg
  • You can't straighten or bend the knee, or its shape is changing quickly
  • Pain in both knees plus fingers or wrists — this may be rheumatoid arthritis
