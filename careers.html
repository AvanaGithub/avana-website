Avana Surgical Systems is a Chennai-based specialist distributor of orthopaedic and spine solutions. Since 2009, we have partnered with over 500 surgeons and supported more than one lakh patients across India.

We are the market leader in endoscopic spine surgery with 250+ installations nationwide, and we continue to grow the orthotic bracing category in India. We are looking for professionals who want to build long-term careers with a specialist organisation.
