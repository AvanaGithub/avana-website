Why this fellowship

Beyond the Operating Room

Learning endoscopic spine surgery is not only about understanding surgical instruments and techniques.

It begins with accurate diagnosis, selecting the right patient, interpreting imaging, planning the surgical approach and managing the patient throughout recovery.

This 45-day fellowship is designed to help surgeons understand the complete clinical and surgical decision-making process involved in Full-Endoscopic Spine Surgery.

With only four delegates per batch, the program aims to provide focused interaction and meaningful opportunities for discussion with the faculty.

What you will learn

Fellowship Learning Objectives

The program is designed to develop understanding in the following areas:

1

Patient Selection & Diagnosis

Indications, contraindications, neurological assessment and MRI correlation.

2

Endoscopic Anatomy & Instruments

Anatomical landmarks, endoscopic systems, surgical instruments and operating-room setup.

3

Transforaminal Endoscopic Surgery

Patient selection, approach planning, access corridors and procedural principles.

4

Interlaminar Endoscopic Surgery

Approach selection, surgical workflow and anatomical considerations.

5

Endoscopic Decompression

Principles of treating lumbar disc herniation, lateral recess stenosis and spinal canal stenosis.

6

Surgical Safety

Complication prevention, recognition, troubleshooting and decision-making.

7

Postoperative Patient Management

Recovery protocols, follow-up assessment and rehabilitation planning.

8

Clinical Case Discussions

Review of imaging, surgical planning and outcomes through faculty-guided discussions.

Program schedule

Proposed Six-Week Learning Structure

Week1

Foundations of Full-Endoscopic Spine Surgery

Clinical assessment, patient selection, spinal anatomy, imaging interpretation and instrumentation.

Week2

Transforaminal Endoscopic Techniques

Approach planning, fluoroscopic landmarks, transforaminal discectomy and foraminoplasty principles.

Week3

Interlaminar Endoscopic Techniques

Interlaminar surgical approach, endoscopic discectomy and workflow-based learning.

Week4

Endoscopic Decompression

Surgical planning for foraminal, lateral recess and central canal stenosis.

Week5

Advanced Endoscopic Spine Surgery

Selected advanced case discussions, including cervical, thoracic and endoscopic fusion techniques, subject to clinical availability.

Week6

Clinical Integration & Patient Management

Preoperative planning, postoperative management, complication discussions and case review.

Final3d

Learning Review

Faculty feedback, case presentations and consolidation of key learning outcomes.

The teaching sequence is proposed and subject to approval by the program faculty.
