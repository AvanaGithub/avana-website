Why 6 weeks

A workshop ends on the third day. Understanding takes longer.

Most endoscopic spine training in India is a 1 to 3-day workshop. You see the instruments. You practise on a model. You go home. This fellowship is built differently.

What you'll build

Patient-selection judgement. Imaging-based decision making. Approach planning. Intraoperative problem-solving. Complication avoidance. Postoperative management. The clinical reasoning that separates a technician from a surgeon.

Who it's for

Orthopaedic surgeons, neurosurgeons and spine surgeons who have completed their postgraduate training and want to do endoscopic spine surgery seriously — not just know what it is.

Where you'll train

Asian Spine Hospital, Hyderabad — India's first dedicated Full Endoscopic Spine Centre.

The fellowship is delivered at Asian Spine Hospital, a 25-bed super-speciality spine centre built around full endoscopic surgery — not a general hospital with an endoscopy list.

The hospital is home to the International Spine Surgery Training & Education Centre (ISSTEC), established by the Spine Endoscopy Research Foundation (SERF) in partnership with Realists Training Technologies (RealSpine), Germany. Fellows train alongside the clinical team and on RealSpine simulation systems for lumbar, thoracic and cervical full endoscopic techniques.

The centre's team has performed over 2,500 full endoscopic spine procedures and more than 5,000 pain procedures, and hosted the 1st World Congress of Spine Endoscopy in Hyderabad in 2019.

How selection works

Fellowship registration & selection process

The fellowship is selective. We want to be sure every fellow has the qualifications, clinical background and commitment to benefit from the program.

Submit Registration

Complete the fellowship registration form with your professional and contact details.

Join the Waiting List

Your application is added to the Fellowship Waiting List once registration is received.

Application Review

The fellowship team reviews your qualifications, clinical experience and interest in FESS.

Interview

Shortlisted applicants are invited for an interview to discuss clinical background and learning objectives.

Selection & Confirmation

Selected candidates receive formal confirmation of dates, fees, joining instructions and documentation requirements.

Registration does not guarantee admission. Final admission is subject to application review, interview, availability of fellowship positions and approval by the fellowship team.
